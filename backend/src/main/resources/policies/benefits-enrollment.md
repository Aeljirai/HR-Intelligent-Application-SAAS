# Benefits & Enrollment

## Health, Dental & Vision
The company offers three health plan tiers (Base, Plus, Premium), plus dental
and vision coverage, all subsidized at 80% of premium cost for employees and
50% for dependents. Coverage begins on your first day of employment once you
complete enrollment.

## Open Enrollment
Open enrollment runs every year in November, with elections taking effect
January 1st of the following year. Outside of open enrollment, you can only
change your elections if you experience a qualifying life event.

## Qualifying Life Events
Marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, loss of other coverage, or
relocation to a new coverage region all open a 30-day special enrollment
window starting from the date of the event.

## 401(k) Retirement Matching
The company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary.
Matching contributions vest immediately — there is no vesting schedule to
wait out. You can adjust your contribution rate at any time through the
benefits portal; changes take effect the following pay cycle.

## Other Benefits
Employees also receive a $75/month wellness stipend (usable toward gym
memberships or the activity clubs in the Wellbeing section), a $500/year
professional development budget, and a $1,000 remote-work equipment stipend
for eligible remote employees (see the Remote Work Policy).
