# PTO & Leave Policy

## Accrual Rules
Every full-time employee accrues 20 PTO days per year, credited monthly at a
rate of roughly 1.67 days/month. Part-time employees accrue a prorated amount
based on scheduled hours. PTO balance and year-to-date usage are always
visible on your personal Dashboard.

## Rollover
Unused PTO days roll over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5
days. Any balance above 5 days remaining on December 31st is forfeited unless
local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting Time Off
Submit a PTO request at least 5 business days in advance through the
Wellbeing or Dashboard tab. Your manager receives a notification and
typically approves within 2 business days. Requests during company blackout
periods (end-of-quarter close weeks) require additional notice.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not require advance notice. Employees
get 10 paid sick days per year; unused sick days do not roll over. A doctor's
note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Employees who have completed 6 months of continuous service are eligible for
12 weeks of paid parental leave (birth, adoption, or foster placement), which
can be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Unpaid Leave of Absence
Employees may request an unpaid leave of absence of up to 90 days for personal
reasons (with HR approval). Benefits continue during an approved unpaid leave
of up to 30 days; beyond that, COBRA continuation rules apply.
