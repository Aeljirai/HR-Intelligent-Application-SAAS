# Payroll & Tax Documents

## Pay Schedule
Employees are paid biweekly via direct deposit. Funds typically post to your
account by 9am on payday. If payday falls on a bank holiday, funds post the
prior business day.

## Updating Direct Deposit
You can update your bank account details in Payroll Settings at any time.
Changes submitted before Wednesday of a given week apply to the next pay
cycle; changes submitted later apply to the cycle after that.

## Pay Stubs
Every pay stub is available for download from the payroll portal
immediately after payday, showing gross pay, deductions, taxes withheld, and
year-to-date totals.

## W-2 and Tax Forms
Your W-2 is issued by January 31st each year and is available for download
from the payroll portal — no request needed. If you worked in multiple states
during the year, a state-specific W-2 is issued for each. 1099 forms for
contractors follow the same January 31st deadline.

## Garnishments & Deductions
Wage garnishments (court-ordered) are processed automatically once payroll
receives the order and are reflected as a line item on your pay stub.
Questions about a specific deduction should go through a Payroll-category
ticket rather than direct messages, so there's a documented record.
