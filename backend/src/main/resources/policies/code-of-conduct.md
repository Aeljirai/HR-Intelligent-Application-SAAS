# Code of Conduct

## Respectful Workplace
Every employee is entitled to a workplace free of harassment, discrimination,
and hostile behavior, regardless of role or seniority. This applies to
in-person, remote, and online interactions (including internal chat and
video calls).

## Reporting a Concern
Concerns can be raised confidentially through the Tickets section (category
"Conduct"), directly with HR, or through the anonymous ethics hotline listed
on the company intranet. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in
good faith is itself a violation of this policy and is grounds for
disciplinary action.

## Investigation Process
HR reviews every conduct report within 2 business days and determines next
steps, which may include a formal investigation. Both the reporting party and
the person(s) involved are kept informed of process (not necessarily outcome
details, which may be confidential) at each stage.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial interest, family relationship, or
outside employment that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with
their role, particularly around hiring, vendor selection, or performance
evaluation decisions.

## Data & Confidentiality
Employee, customer, and company data accessed through work systems may only
be used for legitimate business purposes and must not be shared outside the
company without authorization, including after employment ends.
