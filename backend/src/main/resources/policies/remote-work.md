# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees whose role does not require regular on-site presence (as determined
by their department head) may work remotely on a full-time or hybrid basis.
New hires typically complete their first 30 days on-site or hybrid before
transitioning to a fully remote arrangement, to support onboarding.

## Hybrid Schedules
Hybrid employees are expected to be in the office at least 2 days per week,
coordinated with their team so in-office days overlap. Core collaboration
hours are 10am-3pm in the employee's local time zone, during which employees
should be reachable regardless of location.

## Equipment & Stipend
Fully remote employees receive a one-time $1,000 equipment stipend (desk,
chair, monitor, etc.) and a $50/month connectivity allowance. IT ships
standard equipment (laptop, headset) to any US or Canada address at no cost
to the employee.

## Time Zone Coverage
Teams spanning more than 2 time zones should have at least one recurring
meeting scheduled within a window reasonable for all members; managers are
responsible for rotating meeting times fairly across quarters.

## International Remote Work
Working remotely from outside your home country for more than 30 consecutive
days requires prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax and employment-law
implications. Submit requests at least 4 weeks in advance.
